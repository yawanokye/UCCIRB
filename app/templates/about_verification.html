Safety

What this portal will and will not ask you to do

Applicant accounts

Applicants create an account to submit and manage their own ethical-clearance applications.

Administrative accounts

Administrative users receive accounts created by authorised system administrators and are restricted by role.

Reviewer links

External reviewers use a unique secure assignment link. The reviewer link does not require the reviewer's UCC password.

No software installation

The portal does not require browser extensions, executable downloads, remote-access software or device configuration.

No banking credentials

Login and reviewer pages do not request bank-account numbers, card numbers, PINs or mobile-money PINs.

Verify suspicious communication

If an email or page asks for something outside these functions, stop and verify it with the UCC IRB Secretariat.

3
Independent verification

How to verify the service

  1. Check the browser address bar before entering credentials.
  2. Use the official UCC IRB information site to confirm the Board's contact information.
  3. For a questionable invitation or login page, contact irb@ucc.edu.gh or 0558093143 / 0508878309.
  4. Do not bypass a browser security warning. Report it to the IRB Secretariat or system administrator.
