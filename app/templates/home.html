University of Cape Coast · Institutional Review Board

Ethical Clearance Application and Review Portal

All applications are received centrally by the IRB Secretariat. Applications from the five Colleges with Scientific Committees follow controlled College scientific review, while applications from other UCC academic and administrative units remain with the IRB Secretariat for direct IRB processing.

Application routing

One submission point, two review pathways

IRB Secretariat receives all submissions

College Scientific Committee pathway

These five Colleges have Scientific Committees. After Secretariat screening, the College sees the application, requests access and begins review only after the Secretariat activates the documents.

  • College of Agriculture and Natural Sciences
  • College of Distance Education
  • College of Education Studies
  • College of Humanities and Legal Studies
  • College of Health and Allied Sciences

Direct IRB Secretariat pathway

Applicants from UCC academic or administrative units outside the five Colleges do not pass through a College Scientific Committee. After administrative screening, the Secretariat retains the application and moves it directly to IRB review classification.

Secretariat screening → IRB classification → Ethical review → IRB decision
