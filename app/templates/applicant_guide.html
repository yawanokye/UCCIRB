Application process

How to apply online

1

Read the instructions

Review the UCC-IRB application instructions and download the relevant forms before creating your application.

2

Create an applicant account

Applicants create their own account. Use an email address you can access throughout the review process.

3

Create the application

Enter the research title, applicant type, institutional affiliation, programme and study summary.

4

Upload the application package

Upload the protocol and all documents applicable to your study. Use the document checklist below.

5

Submit to the IRB Secretariat

All applications enter the IRB Secretariat first. Submitted records become locked until the Secretariat requests a correction or a review stage requires revision.

6

Respond and track

Monitor your dashboard, respond to review comments and upload revised versions when requested.

2
Application package

Documents required to apply

RequiredApplication letter for ethical clearance by the Principal Investigator

Upload a signed application letter addressed for ethical clearance.

RequiredResearch protocol / completed UCC-IRB Composite Form

Prepare the proposal in the UCC-IRB format and ensure all applicable sections are completed.

StudentsSupervisor and institutional support letter(s)

Student applicants should provide the required supervisor and Head of College/School/Faculty/Department support documentation.

As applicableConsent form(s), assent form and checklist

Use the adult consent, child assent or records-keeper form according to the study population and source of data.

As applicableData collection instrument(s)

Attach questionnaires, interview guides, observation schedules or other study instruments where used.

RequiredSimilarity report

The UCC-IRB instructions state that protocols are subject to a similarity threshold of not more than 15%.

RequiredAbridged Curriculum Vitae

Use the UCC-IRB CV template. Student investigators should also include the supervisor's abridged CV where required.

Non-studentsCollaborator support letter(s)

Where collaborators are from a different institution, include the relevant support documentation.

Tip: Download the official resources below before preparing your final upload package.
3
Before submission

Important information

Complete every applicable section

Incomplete applications may be returned during Secretariat administrative screening.

Do not begin primary data collection first

The UCC-IRB instructions state that investigators who have already started or gathered primary data are not eligible to apply for ethical clearance.

Use clear document formatting

The current UCC-IRB instructions specify A4 paper and clear fonts such as Times New Roman, Arial or Calibri.

Respond to review comments promptly

The uploaded instructions state that applications may be withdrawn if review comments are not addressed within one month.

After submission

What happens next?

Your application is received by the UCC-IRB Secretariat and checked for completeness. From your applicant account, you can follow the status, respond to comments, upload revisions when requested, and download released review reports or final clearance documents.
